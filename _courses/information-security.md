This course runs on a simple weekly rhythm — a **live lecture**, quick **interactive
questions during class**, and a short **graded quiz at the end of each session** — and
then every material stays available in the Course App so students can review and track
their own progress.

### How each class works

1. **Live lecture** — the week's core ideas, grounded in real examples.
2. **Think during class** — short questions and live pulses keep the session interactive.
3. **End-of-class quiz** — a quick graded quiz in the Course App checks the day's topic and counts toward the final grade.
4. **Review any time** — decks, practice missions, and personal results stay available so students can revisit weak spots.

</section>
