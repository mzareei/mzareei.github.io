This course is being shaped as a sequence of short security missions. Each week
combines a live lecture deck, a quick quiz pulse, and a hands-on decision task
so students repeatedly practice thinking like defenders and attackers.

<section class="course-mission-hero" markdown="1">

### Learning flow

1. **Frame the mission** — what can go wrong, who is affected, and why it matters.
2. **Make decisions during lecture** — quick checks, anonymous votes, and live quiz pulses.
3. **Practice with a micro-lab** — short browser-based activities that require a security judgment.
4. **Review weak spots** — quiz results and missed concepts guide what to revisit.

The live classroom tools are designed for short rhythm changes during lecture:
project a QR code, run a two-minute decision pulse, then debrief the reasoning
before students return to the main explanation.
