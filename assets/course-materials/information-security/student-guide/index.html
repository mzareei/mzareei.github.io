How To Use The Course Platform

Use this page when you need to know what to open, what to submit, and how your progress is recorded.

Start here

The short version

In class, scan the QR code from your teacher. Use the same Student ID every time. Complete the quiz or activity, submit the exit ticket, then check My Progress and Review Coach after class.

In class

Checklist

  1. Scan the QR code from the screen.
  2. Enter your name and Student ID.
  3. Use the same Student ID every time.
  4. Answer the quiz or activity on your phone.
  5. Submit before the timer ends.
After class

Checklist

  1. Open My Progress.
  2. Read the Next 7 days study plan.
  3. Run Review Coach for at least five cards.
  4. Complete any mission or case file marked as weak.
  5. Submit an exit ticket if you did not do it in class.
Before evaluation

Checklist

  1. Open Learning Portfolio.
  2. Check that your name and Student ID are correct.
  3. Review the evidence summary.
  4. Download JSON or CSV if your teacher asks for it.
  5. Press Submit record if your teacher uses Supabase portfolio collection.
Important

Your Student ID

Your Student ID connects your quiz attempts, exit tickets, and portfolio record. If you use different IDs, your teacher may see your work as separate people.

Use your official school ID. Do not use a nickname, initials, or a different format each week.

Where your work is saved

On your browser

Progress, review cards, and local practice are saved in this browser. If you switch device or clear browser data, local progress may not follow.

In Supabase

Live quiz submissions, exit tickets, and submitted portfolio records may be saved for your teacher when the page is connected.
