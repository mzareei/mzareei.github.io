How To Use The Course App

Everything for this course lives in one place. Sign in with your institutional email and follow each week as your teacher releases it.

5 steps
Start here

The short version

Open the Course App and sign in with your @tec.mx email — you get a one-time link (or a 6-digit code), no password. In class, open the released lecture, take the quiz when your teacher starts it, and submit the exit ticket. After class, check My Progress. Your grades and work save automatically under your sign-in.

Signing in

Your institutional email

  1. Open the Course App.
  2. Enter your official @tec.mx email.
  3. Open the sign-in link we email you (or type the 6-digit code).
  4. That's it — no password, no Student ID to remember.

Your institutional email is your identity for grades. There is nothing else to type each week.

During the quiz

Fair play

  1. Quizzes are timed and usually one attempt.
  2. Answer on your own device and stay on the quiz tab.
  3. The app records timing and tab activity for integrity.
  4. Submit before the timer ends.

Using an AI assistant to answer graded quizzes is not allowed — and it is detectable.

After class

Keep up

  1. Open My Progress.
  2. Review your scores and weak topics.
  3. Use Review Coach and re-open the released materials.
  4. Submit any exit ticket you missed.
