## Short bio

Mahdi Zareei is a Research Professor at the School of Engineering and Sciences,
Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico. His research focuses on information security,
applied machine learning, and natural language processing. He is a Senior Member
of the IEEE and a member of Mexico's National System of Researchers (SNI, Level I),
and serves as an Associate Editor for several international journals.

## Full biography

Mahdi Zareei (M'17 – SM'20) received the M.Sc. degree in computer network from the
University of Science, Malaysia, in 2011, and the Ph.D. degree from the Communication
Systems and Networks Research Group, Malaysia-Japan International Institute of
Technology, University of Technology, Malaysia, in 2016. In 2017, he joined the
School of Engineering and Sciences, Tecnológico de Monterrey, as a Postdoctoral
Fellow, where since 2019 he has worked as a Research Professor.

His research mainly focuses on information security, applied machine learning, and
natural language processing, building on earlier work in wireless sensor networks,
cognitive radio networks, and communication protocols. He is a member of the Mexican
National Researchers System (SNI, Level I) and a Senior Member of the IEEE.

He serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Access, PLOS One, and the Ad Hoc & Sensor
Wireless Networks journals, and reviews for several leading venues in computer
networks and communications.
